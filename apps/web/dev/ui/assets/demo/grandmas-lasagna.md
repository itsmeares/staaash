# Grandma's lasagna

Serves 6.

- 12 sheets of pasta
- 500 g beef and pork mince
- 700 ml passata
- 250 g ricotta
- 200 g mozzarella
- A handful of basil

1. Brown the mince, add the passata, and simmer for an hour.
2. Layer sauce, pasta, and ricotta three times. Finish with mozzarella.
3. Bake at 190 °C for 40 minutes. Let it rest for 10 before cutting.
