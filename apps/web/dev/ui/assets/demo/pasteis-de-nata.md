# Pastéis de nata

From the bakery in Belém. Makes 12.

- 1 roll of puff pastry
- 6 egg yolks
- 250 ml cream
- 120 g sugar
- Lemon peel and a cinnamon stick

1. Roll the pastry into a log, slice it, and press each slice into a tin.
2. Warm the cream with the sugar, peel, and cinnamon. Whisk in the yolks.
3. Fill the tins and bake at 280 °C for 9 minutes, until the tops blister.
