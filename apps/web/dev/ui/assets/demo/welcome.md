# Welcome to Staaash

This is the de Vries household's demo drive. It runs entirely in your browser,
so nothing you upload leaves your computer.

Some things to try:

- Drag a few files or a whole folder onto the page to upload them.
- Open **Home renovation** and use the arrow keys to flip through the photos.
- Open a PDF in **Documents**, or the homelab notes in **Homelab**.
- Right-click a file to rename, move, favorite, or share it.
- Share a folder, then open the link in a new tab to see what a visitor sees.
- Delete something, then bring it back from **Trash**.
- Visit **Admin** to see the household, storage, and background jobs.

When you are ready to run your own, Staaash needs Docker, PostgreSQL, and a
disk. Use **Reset demo** at the top to start over.
