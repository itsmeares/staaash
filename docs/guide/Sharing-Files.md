# Sharing Files

A share link lets someone open a file or folder without an account. You choose how long it works, and you can turn it off at any time.

## Share something

1. Find the file or folder in your drive.
2. Open its menu and choose **Share**.
3. Press **Create link**, then **Copy link**. Send it to the person.

Anyone with the link can open it. Treat the link like a key.

## Choose how it works

In the share window you can set:

- **Expires.** When the link stops working. Pick a preset or choose a date and time. Your owner sets the longest time a link may last.
- **Password.** Turn it on and type a password. People must type it to open the link.
- **Allow downloads.** Turn it off for a view-only link. People can look but not download.

## Share a folder

People who open a folder link can browse the folder and the folders inside it. They cannot see anything outside it.

## See and manage your links

Open **Shared** in the sidebar. It lists your links. From there you can change the expiry or turn a link off. **Settings** has a **Manage** button for share links too.

## Turn a link off

Open the file's share window and press the turn-off button, or find the link in **Shared** and turn it off. Staaash asks "Turn off this link?". After you confirm, anyone who has the link can no longer open it. Your files stay where they are.

## What visitors see

A visitor sees a simple page with the file or folder. Photos and videos open inline. If a link expired or was turned off, the visitor sees a message saying so.

## Links that use your domain name

Share links use the address you open Staaash from. If you run Staaash behind a domain, set `STAAASH_PUBLIC_URL` so links always use it. See [[Reverse Proxy]].
